## News/releases

- **Missed coverage: .NET 11 ships; check your WinUI app’s runtime-support plan.** Microsoft .NET Blog, November 10, published 08:11 PST—19 minutes before yesterday’s cutoff, but not previously included. Microsoft reiterates WinUI 3 as its recommended native framework for new Windows desktop apps, while continuing WPF and WinForms development. The practical lifecycle change: .NET 8 and 9 reached end of support November 10; .NET 11 offers two-year STS support, with .NET 10 the LTS alternative. Installing the SDK does not retarget an app or upgrade Windows App SDK packages automatically; validate native dependencies and your real publishing mode. This is a .NET release, **not a new WinUI release**. Direct SDK-release inspection found nothing newer than the already-covered November 7 experimental package.
  <https://devblogs.microsoft.com/dotnet/announcing-dotnet-11/>

## microsoft-ui-xaml — past 24 hours

**Exact window:** November 10, 2026, 08:30 PST → November 11, 2026, 08:30 PST. Direct authenticated API inspection covered **36 issues across all states**, with PRs excluded. The substantive updates below are **Issues**, not Discussions.

- **ComboBox: new variable-row crash repro.** Opened November 11, tipa reports an SDK 2.6.5 layout-cycle termination when scrolling rows that vary in both height and width. The author supplies standalone code and traces, reports nine successful local reproductions and 19 production crashes, and finds uniform row heights avoid the problem; uniform widths also avoid the supplied repro. Those are author-reported results, not independently verified counts or a maintainer-confirmed diagnosis. Useful immediate mitigation for affected pickers: normalize row dimensions while testing your own data.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12637>

- **GPU retention: new detach/reattach trigger and workaround.** In a November 11, 08:22 PST update to the November 7 CanvasImageSource issue, tipa reports roughly 9 MiB retained per map-panel remove/re-add cycle—224→579 MiB across 40 switches. Replacing the Image/surface did not help; keeping the panel attached, including collapsed-and-redrawn, plateaued in the author’s tests. This extends the earlier move/delayed-redraw report with a concrete lifecycle mitigation, not an upstream fix.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12606#issuecomment-6801164565>

- **Path rendering: verified source fix merged.** The February 2024 regression issue closed November 10 after PR #12460 merged. The patch changes scaled-edge rounding for alpha-mask dimensions and offsets when texture limits apply, with added scaling/texture-limit tests. This addresses clipped antialiased Path edges at elevated display scaling; applications affected should track the eventual containing package. **Merged source verified; package shipment not established.**
  <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/9338>
  Patch: <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/pull/12460>

- **CsWinRT 3 preview migration hits missing managed types.** New November 10 report: a minimal .NET 10 class library targeting `net10.0-windows10.0.26100.1`, SDK 2.6.5 and CsWinRT 3 preview restores but fails to resolve `Microsoft.UI.Xaml.FrameworkElement` with CS0234. A minimal project and CI evidence are linked. Treat this as a preview-combination compatibility warning, not proof that ordinary WinUI projects are broken; the packaging explanation remains the reporter’s hypothesis, with no confirmed fix.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12625>

- **Experimental Chart clipping: diagnosis advances, delivery unverified.** On the October 20 issue, contributor Nitin-100 explained November 10 Pacific that shared category axes place edge columns on tick marks, clipping the first/last columns. The contributor says an internal PR moves categories between ticks and passes 479 tests. That is useful diagnostic progress, but the internal review and test claims cannot be publicly verified; do not treat it as merged or shipped.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12350#issuecomment-6788746887>

- **TOPMOST disruption: narrowly scoped hosting guidance.** A November 10 reply to the September 2024 issue recommends creating/attaching a custom `DesktopWindowXamlSource` during `WM_CREATE`, rather than `WM_NCCREATE`. The contributor explicitly scopes this to custom XAML-island hosts—not ordinary WinUI Window hosting. It is a suggested workaround, not a framework fix or independently tested remedy.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/9990#issuecomment-6775574306>
