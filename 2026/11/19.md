## Community experience

- **Reactor goes cross-platform through Uno—not a portable Windows App SDK.** Reddit r/csharp, November 19. Martin Zikmund’s fork recompiles Microsoft.UI.Reactor against Uno’s WinUI-compatible APIs; documentation confirms preview desktop/web/mobile support but excludes the WinAppSDK target. Readable replies show interest alongside MVU/MVVM questions and skepticism about Microsoft’s staying power; the author distinguishes free/open-source dependencies from paid Studio tooling. This is experimental, Skia-rendered Uno off Windows, with gaps including docking and DevTools—not “native WinUI 3 everywhere.” <https://www.reddit.com/r/csharp/comments/1y07rjg/got_microsoftuireactor_to_run_crossplatform/> · <https://platform.uno/docs/articles/features/using-reactor.html>

- **FluidCalc applies WinUI 3 to offline engineering tools.** Reddit r/windowsapps, November 18, 08:02 PST; **missed-coverage catch-up**, just before yesterday’s cutoff. Its student developer reports a Store launch of a C#/Windows App SDK HVAC utility with an interactive psychrometric chart and thermal/pipe-sizing calculators, offering a free core and one-time Pro unlock. A concrete adoption example; offline behavior, responsiveness and calculation accuracy were not independently tested. <https://www.reddit.com/r/windowsapps/comments/1xziwif/i_built_fluidcalc_a_modern_native_c_winui_3/>

- **Yesterday’s VS template problem gets a merged packaging fix.** WindowsAppSDK PR #6986, merged November 18 at 13:04 PST. It restores seven C# WinUI item templates in the VSIX and excludes preview Reactor templates; source-branch validation checked visibility and placeholder expansion. This advances yesterday’s #6968 report, but **no distributed VSIX/Visual Studio fix was verified**. <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/pull/6986>

- **Windowless-host hangs gain fresh deployment evidence.** WindowsAppSDK #6797, opened September 24; new report November 18. The reporter says runtime-2.7.0 redeployment through `winapp run` took about four minutes and generated seven hang reports, including Microsoft apps. A November 19 contributor reply in linked #5870 says completed background-task hosts are not expected to remain alive for future activations; cause and fix remain unresolved, not a confirmed 2.7 regression. <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/6797#issuecomment-6992453244> · <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/5870#issuecomment-7006704477>

## News/releases

- **FluentCleaner ships its NativeAOT build beyond preview.** Release 26.11.108, November 19. Its developer reports startup dropping from 347 to 126 ms and unpacked size from 118 to 17 MB; these are author measurements, with unused AI-library removal also contributing to size savings—not a universal WinUI benchmark. The practical lesson: one remaining reflection-dependent language list broke in the preview and required correction; settings/inventory had already moved to generated code. Today’s release also adopts stock WinUI styles and exposes Mica/Mica Alt/Acrylic selection. <https://github.com/builtbybel/FluentCleaner/releases/tag/26.11.108> · <https://github.com/builtbybel/FluentCleaner/releases/tag/26.11.99>

## microsoft-ui-xaml — past 24 hours

**Exact window:** November 18, 2026, 08:30 PST → November 19, 2026, 08:30 PST. Direct API inspection covered **21 all-state issues**, excluding PRs, and all **650 Discussion trees**. Discussions are enabled: one new Discussion and its fresh reader response were verified. Highlights:

- **Discussion: TableView hierarchy, context menus and live shaping explained.** New #12762, November 19. A team member supplies Contoso Sales Ledger examples, including parent/child rows and notification-driven sorting/filtering; background updates should use the UI dispatcher. Reader feedback reiterates selection/MVVM limitations and requests maintainable sample repositories. This uses the **already-released experimental package**, not a new stable control. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/discussions/12762>

- **Issue: important correction to yesterday’s TableViewEx workaround.** #12730, opened November 17; substantive comment edit November 19. DHancock removed `ContainerFromItem`: realizing arbitrary containers outside the cache range caused unpredictable subsequent scrolling. `SelectItem` now validates selection and returns a success boolean. Revisit copied code; this remains a simple-grid community workaround, not an upstream fix. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12730#issuecomment-6959634228>

- **Issue: TitleBar teardown can crash while its window survives.** New #12754, November 18. The reporter describes production access violations on self-contained 2.6.5 and says clearing `IconSource` before releasing the TitleBar stopped the deterministic crash. **Reporter-tested mitigation only**; proposed PR #12760 remains unmerged, without full build/regression-test validation. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12754>

- **Issue: `Loaded` does not guarantee pointer readiness.** New #12753, November 18. Kenny Kerr’s timing repro loses the first pointer move in 29/30 trials at 10 ms after loading, versus 0/30 for WinForms. A linked windows-rs test-driver change waits for observed pointer entry and retries moves, reportedly passing 100/100 CI trials: **automation mitigation, not a WinUI framework fix**. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12753>

- **Issue: new XAML can become `None` instead of `Page`.** New #12758, November 18 Pacific. Morten’s import-order repro explains how JSON item templates can skip generated code/XBF and cause missing `InitializeComponent`. Moving removal later helps locally, but blanket removal breaks intentional project customizations. Distinct from the merged VSIX fix above; **no complete upstream fix verified**. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12758>
