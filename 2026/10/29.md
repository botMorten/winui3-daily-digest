## Community experience

- **Windows 10 islands: shared-runtime update reportedly makes GDI content transparent.** WindowsAppSDK #6909, October 28. A shipping MFC app and official WinForms-islands sample reportedly break after the installed runtime changes from 2.3.1 to 2.6.4, although the app still references SDK 2.3.1; the WinUI island itself remains correct. A contributor proposes disabling the host accent in a queued low-priority dispatcher callback after island initialization. This is **unconfirmed diagnosis/workaround**, observed on Windows 10 Hyper-V VMs; physical GPUs and fully patched Windows 10 remain untested. <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/6909> · <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/6909#issuecomment-6478773773>

- **ARM64EC—not native ARM64 generally—gets a 2.6.4 startup warning.** WindowsAppSDK #6911, October 29. A repository member reports `0xC0000139` because two ARM64EC DLLs import `USER32!TransformPoint` by name rather than ordinal; corresponding native ARM64/x64 binaries use ordinal imports. There is no released fix. The suggested 2.5.1 fallback is **explicitly not runtime-validated** in the investigation, so treat it as a testing lead, not a guaranteed remedy. <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/6911>

- **Missed coverage: Mole shares design rules rather than forcing one cross-platform UI.** Tw93, October 22. The developer describes a separate C#/WinUI 3/.NET 10 rewrite, with scripts converting Swift design tokens into XAML resources and synchronizing localization. Core/ViewModel tests run on Mac; WinUI builds and visual checks use Windows in Parallels, while physical hardware covers installers, HiDPI and SmartScreen. Self-contained x64/ARM64 installers and Velopack updates simplify delivery, but signing-session renewal complicates automation. The product site corroborates the separate native implementation; performance and testing claims remain the author’s account. <https://tw93.fun/en/2026-10-22/desktop.html> · <https://mole.fit/windows>

- **Old localization bug, fresh template-dependency warning.** WindowsAppSDK #5817, originally a 2025 report; new October 29 comment. A developer says VS2026’s blank packaged WinUI template still resolves outdated MSIX build tools associated with lost bundled languages. An earlier contributor identified a May 13 tooling fix. Check the **resolved packaging-tool dependency**, rather than assuming a new runtime regression; neither the current template nor the proposed remedy was independently tested here. <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/5817#issuecomment-6499828077>

## microsoft-ui-xaml — past 24 hours

**Exact window: October 28, 08:30–October 29, 08:30 PDT.** Issues and Discussions checked directly, including older threads’ new comments/replies.

- **Hybrid-GPU corruption: six fresh human comments broaden yesterday’s report.** Older #12434 gains MAUI and Notepad reproductions on October 28–29. One developer’s NVIDIA preference helps; another’s Intel preference helps—**no universal “use high performance” prescription**. New #12464 adds mixed-DPI repros affecting stable 2.3.1/2.6.4 but not the reporter’s experimental comparison. Matching scaling avoids that repro; a shared root cause and shipped fix are unproven. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12434> · <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12464>

- **Windows 10 dragging/resizing report gets an important maintainer correction.** New #12471, October 29, reports breakage on 2.6.4/Windows build 18362. The maintainer says the older title-bar fix blamed by the reporter **has not shipped in stable**, cannot reproduce on a different Windows 10 build, and requests version comparisons. Serious report, unresolved cause—not proof that a merged PR caused a released regression. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12471#issuecomment-6499520937>

- **Experimental TableView: insertion stalls and retained grouped data.** New October 29 reports #12472–#12474 include a roughly three-minute UI stall for 10,000 sorted/filtered additions and growing retained sources/items across repeated navigation. Detaching shaping before bulk insertion helps particular tests; clearing `GroupBy` helps the reported lifetime case, but is not a universal sort/filter remedy. These are human measurements against experimental/main code, **not stable-control regressions or shipped fixes**. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12472> · <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12473> · <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12474>

- **Minimized-first TitleBar startup has a focused crash repro.** New #12459, October 28, reports failures on 2.5.1 and 2.6.4 when first showing a TitleBar-equipped window minimized. The reporter’s workaround is first laying out while hidden, then minimizing through a low-priority dispatcher callback. Relevant to tray/minimized-launch apps; no maintainer-confirmed fix yet. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12459>
