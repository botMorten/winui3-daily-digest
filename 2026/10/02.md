# WinUI 3 developer-community digest — October 2, 2026
**Coverage:** October 1, 08:30 → October 2, 08:30 PDT. Seven-day catch-up searches; previously reported stories excluded. Overall coverage is partial.

## Takeaways
- **Packaging gets tangible fixes:** a published MSIX BuildTools update addresses resource/publish failures—not merely a promised source change.
- **Practical gotchas:** scope `WindowsAppSDKSelfContained` to the executable, and don’t equate successful notification calls with visible toasts.
- **Input performance:** fresh ColorPicker reports implicate high mouse polling rates; users provide a mitigation and debugging evidence, not a confirmed framework fix.

## Community experience
- **SimpleFiles: a student ships a native file manager.** Reddit r/SideProject, October 1. The author describes a C#/WinUI 3/.NET 8 app now free in the Store, with spacebar previews, targeted installer cleanup and encrypted media streaming without temporary decrypted files. A concrete adoption example; privacy/performance claims are the author’s, not independently tested, and no substantive comments were visible.
  <https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/comments/1wv2qp5/simplefiles_a_lightweight_native_winui_3_file/>

- **Update: missing unpackaged toasts get a tested workaround.** WindowsAppSDK #6821, opened September 29; new test October 1. A participant reports that setting a fixed process AUMID **and** launching through a Start-menu shortcut carrying matching AUMID/ToastActivatorCLSID metadata made the official sample display a banner. Explicit AUMID alone had failed; shortcut-only sufficiency remains untested. This is a single-machine workaround—not a maintainer-confirmed fix.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/6821#issuecomment-5935391495>

- **Self-contained publishing: scope the property, don’t await an SDK fix.** WindowsAppSDK #6365, opened March 31; conclusion October 1. Contributor Felipe G closed the report as by design: command-line `-p:WindowsAppSDKSelfContained=true` propagates across the project graph, including class libraries, while the property is application-only. Put it in the executable project or publish profile; a clearer error message is planned.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/6365#issuecomment-5942289721>

- **WAP-to-MSIX tooling migration advances; preview still pending.** WindowsAppSDK #6353, opened March 27; new exchange October 1 PDT. Felipe G says a `PackageSubfolder` prototype remains under review and addresses WinRT metadata placement and incorrect in-process-server manifest entries alongside output collisions. The developer is eager to test it; mixed COM-server/background-task projects should treat this as migration work in progress, not functionality already delivered by today’s package.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/6353#issuecomment-5944369568>

## News/releases
- **MSIX BuildTools 1.7.260930100 ships specific WinUI deployment fixes.** NuGet, October 1; contributor confirmations October 1. The published package is identified as fixing long-path PRI expansion failures, omitted XBF/PRI files during unpackaged NativeAOT publishing, missing published PRI resources without `EnableMsixTooling`, and redundant library XBF files in MSIX payloads. These affect CI reliability, startup and package contents; availability is verified, but the repros were not independently rerun.
  <https://www.nuget.org/packages/Microsoft.Windows.SDK.BuildTools.MSIX/1.7.260930100>
  Confirmations: <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/6795#issuecomment-5936125326> · <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/6394#issuecomment-5936188240> · <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/3718#issuecomment-5936402419> · <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/6605#issuecomment-5936190617>

## microsoft-ui-xaml — past 24 hours
**Exact window: October 1, 08:30 → October 2, 08:30 PDT.** All entries below are **Issues**, including older threads with new comments.

- **ColorPicker lag: another polling-rate confirmation (#11243).** In this July thread, three new comments came from three users; two supply technical evidence. One confirms lowering mouse polling rate immediately removes their lag; another reports roughly 20,000 pending messages at 1,000 Hz and repeated list scans. Check high-polling-rate mice when reproducing stalls; this remains user debugging, not a maintainer-confirmed cause or shipped fix.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/11243#issuecomment-5937145174> · <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/11243#issuecomment-5945789995>

- **Gamepad routing gets fresh Microsoft confirmation (#12141).** A Microsoft member opened and closed a tracking issue October 1, linking the source change for opt-in `GamepadKeyRouting` through keyboard/focus navigation on supported Windows 11 24H2+ systems. Useful for controller-first apps, but the PR merged September 29; **today’s change is confirmation, not verified package shipment**.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12141#issuecomment-5940237152>

- **New fullscreen/titlebar one-pixel-gap repro (#12138).** Filed October 1: `FullScreen` plus `ExtendsContentIntoTitleBar=true` leaves a top-edge gap. The author explicitly distinguishes work on `main` from unconfirmed reproduction on the listed 2.5.1 package. A focused kiosk/fullscreen test case—not an established stable-release regression.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12138>

- **Async runtime-XAML loading requested (#12142).** Filed October 2: a `XamlReader.LoadAsync` proposal cites UI-thread stalls and an author-measured approximately 5 ms load for a simple Window/Button. No maintainer commitment or implementation exists; the measurement is not independently verified.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12142>

- **Update: TableView crash thread turns to packaging questions (#12115).** Two October 1 comments from two participants question separate Tabular/Charts DLLs, manual style imports and Chart source visibility. This adds integration concerns—not a new workaround, resolution or shipped fix; yesterday’s self-contained workaround is not repeated as new.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12115#issuecomment-5935113810>
