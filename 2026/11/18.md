## Community experience

- **VS 18.12 breaks the UserControl template.** WindowsAppSDK #6968, opened November 12; fresh confirmation November 17 Pacific. Morten (`dotMorten`) reproduced the upgrade regression: generated XAML and C# retain the literal `__WINUI_USERCONTROL__` placeholder. His follow-up identifies a likely originating change and says newer template-deployment work avoids it, but those templates have **not shipped**—a tooling failure, not evidence that your control implementation is broken. <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/6968#issuecomment-6965820478>

- **Completed background tasks can still leave package updates hanging.** WindowsAppSDK #6797, opened September 24; new evidence November 17. The reporter tested three apps in Windows Sandbox: after each task completed its deferral, updating its still-running windowless host took about 31 seconds and generated `MoAppHang`; a hidden window reduced updates to under half a second. This extends the earlier widget-host report to `IBackgroundTask` hosts, but remains reporter-tested mitigation—not a maintainer-confirmed shutdown contract or proof of the cause of all production hangs. <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/6797#issuecomment-6962862187>

- **kDrive exposes a production FolderPicker edge case.** WindowsAppSDK #6987, November 18. Its developer reports `PickSingleFolderAsync` throwing `E_INVALIDARG` on one Windows Server 2019 machine when selecting folders on a secondary NTFS volume, while C: works; the unpackaged, self-contained app uses SDK 1.8.6. Source links and detailed exclusions make this a useful diagnostic lead, but neither an SDK-wide regression nor a workaround is established. <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/6987>

- **Osaurus 1.0: native WinUI 3 adoption meets model-storage friction.** Reddit r/windowsapps, November 13 launch **catch-up**, with new support replies November 18. The developer describes a free x64/ARM64 local/cloud AI app; an overnight user could not relocate downloads from a full C: drive, and the developer pointed to the Models Folder setting without a confirmed resolution. Readable comments include design praise and source/trust concerns, but these are individual reactions—not representative sentiment or verified privacy guarantees. <https://www.reddit.com/r/windowsapps/comments/1xvetn3/osaurus_a_free_native_app_for_running_ai_on_your/>

- **ImageGlass award resurfaces a useful WinUI-versus-Avalonia migration account.** Avalonia’s November 13 Port Challenge winners announcement, **catch-up**. ImageGlass won Best Everyday Tool; its roughly 60%-complete WinUI 3 attempt remains available alongside the Avalonia implementation. The linked migration retrospective is **September 18**, not new: its author describes window/DPI/elevation friction in WinUI and GPU-memory/custom-rendering work in Avalonia—valuable trade-offs, not an independent benchmark or one-sided victory lap. <https://avaloniaui.net/blog/avalonia-port-challenge-winners>

## News/releases

- **Windows App SDK 2.7.0 is stable, with concrete WinUI fixes.** Microsoft, November 17. Alongside compatible-GPU LanguageModel inference and clearer low-VRAM/Game Mode failures, it ships fixes for empty text-control cursor indicators, DPI-offset input with the System Composition Engine, optimized-style dialog animations/flyout tooltips, default ScrollBar exceptions, shutdown-time `Window.DispatcherQueue` access and ARM64EC startup. These are released fixes: target the applicable scenarios in upgrade tests rather than assuming every longstanding WinUI problem is resolved. <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/releases/tag/v2.7.0>

## microsoft-ui-xaml — past 24 hours

**Exact window:** November 17, 2026, 08:30 PST → November 18, 2026, 08:30 PST. Direct API inspection covered 35 all-state issues, with PRs excluded from that count, and complete Discussion comment/reply trees. Discussions are enabled; **no in-window Discussion activity was found**. Highlights below are **Issues**, including older threads with fresh activity.

- **MenuBar replacement now updates the flyout—in source.** #12742, opened November 17 Pacific; fix merged November 18 at 05:42 PST. Replacing an item through `SetAt`/the indexer could leave the old object displayed; PR #12743 adds the missing replacement handling and a regression test. **Merged source fix, not a verified packaged shipment**, and later than 2.7.0’s publication. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12742>

- **NavigationView’s disappearing selection indicator gets a source fix.** #9812, opened July 2024; collaborator-authored PR #12328 merged November 18 at 04:27 PST and closed the issue. It restores indicator state across pane-mode/template changes, but explicitly leaves a first-Top-switch case involving data-bound children of collapsed parents unresolved. Again: **source merged, not included in the already-published 2.7.0 release**. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/9812>

- **TableView gets a bounded community workaround.** #12730, opened November 17; new reply that day, edited November 18. DHancock’s `TableViewEx` adds item/index/selection helpers through `PART_RowsRepeater`, plus a deferred second bring-into-view call for the first unrealized-row scrolling problem. Explicitly limited to simple grids without grouping/hierarchy; **not an upstream API fix**. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12730#issuecomment-6959634228>

- **Reentrancy-crash follow-up carefully stops short of claiming a fix.** #11099, opened May 12; new production disposition November 17. The reporter shipped with diagnostic ComWrappers table activity disabled, but explicitly demonstrated neither prevention nor measurable crash reduction; other locking paths remain. Treat this as application-level risk acceptance, not a general mitigation recommendation or framework resolution. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/11099#issuecomment-6958045043>
