## Community experience

- **Store-update hangs: a hidden window does not cover suspended background processes.** WindowsAppSDK issue #6797, opened September 24; new developer report October 12. <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/6797#issuecomment-6122192571>
  A second publisher reports release-day Partner Center hang rates rising from roughly 0.8% to 12% of devices. Unlike the earlier windowless reproduction, its resident packaged process already has a hidden top-level window and shutdown handlers; the new probe suggests Modern Standby suspension prevents it from processing deployment’s close request, after which Windows kills it and records `MoAppHang`. The publisher’s controlled test found `uap17:UpdateWhileInUse=defer` prevented forced updating of the suspended probe, but Store retry semantics and support on older Windows remain unanswered. **Practical implication:** test servicing while the app is suspended, not just while its message loop is running. These are publisher-reported measurements and a proposed explanation—not a Microsoft-confirmed root cause or shipped fix.

- **On-device image descriptions expose two different accessibility failure modes.** WindowsAppSDK #6855, opened October 11, plus new October 11–12 replies on #6852, opened October 10. <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/6855> · <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/6852#issuecomment-6114834963>
  A developer building descriptions for blind and low-vision users reports `ImageHasTooMuchText` refusals for 18 of 216 app screenshots, including ordinary dialogs, across theme/scale variants; cropping described some portions but is not an established app-level solution. Separately, Microsoft’s es0m-msft reproduced corrupted successful descriptions and says that defect is already fixed internally and should become public in the next release. The fixing package/build remains unspecified, and that statement **does not resolve the screenshot-refusal issue**. This is Windows App SDK AI/Windows Copilot Runtime coverage, not a WinUI XAML-control defect; developers integrating it should handle both refusal statuses and misleadingly successful output.

## microsoft-ui-xaml — past 24 hours

**Exact window:** October 11, 08:30:22 → October 12, 08:30:22 PDT. Issues and Discussions were checked directly, including older threads with new activity.

- **NavigationView keyboard behavior: conflicting retests, not a blanket fix.** Issues #11049 (opened March 28, 2026) and #5744 (August 21, 2021); new replies October 12. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/11049#issuecomment-6122453072> · <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/5744#issuecomment-6122123195>
  After a collaborator could not reproduce arrow-key tab skipping, the reporter confirmed it still occurs in Gallery 2.9.3.0 with SDK 2.5. Separately, the collaborator closed the older multiple-SelectionChanged-events issue as no longer reproducible on 2.5.1. Those are different defects: test your specific keyboard-navigation scenario rather than interpreting one closure as a general resolution.

- **Reusable Flyout roots hit a compiled-binding boundary.** New issue #12231, October 12. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12231>
  A developer provides C++/WinRT steps, and reports a C# reproduction, for `WMC1125` when a standalone XAML-backed Flyout uses `x:Bind` on 2.5.1. The compiler requires a FrameworkElement or Window root. Wrapping visual content in a UserControl or switching to runtime Binding are architectural alternatives cited by the reporter—not equivalent fixes or a maintainer-approved resolution.

- **Hide-and-restore apps: custom Close-button hover can stick.** New issue #12232, October 12. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12232>
  A minimal sample cancels closing, hides the window, and restores the same instance; its Close button remains red until the pointer enters again. The reporter reproduced it on 2.3.1 and 2.5.1; a standard title bar, or using Alt+F4 instead of clicking X, avoided the symptom in their comparisons. No maintainer diagnosis or framework fix is established.

- **ScrollView overflow proposal targets clipped shadows and scaled cards.** New issue #12233, October 12. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12233>
  The proposed `CanContentRenderOutsideBounds` property would bring ScrollViewer-like opt-out clipping to ScrollView/ScrollPresenter. New activity links an open implementation and API-spec PR; defaults aim to preserve existing rendering, while app-assigned clips and explicit corner radii need careful handling. This is proposed functionality, not an API available in a published SDK.

- **ContentIcon debate has a concrete shipping-app motivation.** Issue #12215, opened October 8; two new community replies October 11. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12215#issuecomment-6115286514>
  One participant argues for a single UIElement to limit complexity and bugs. Files-app designer mdtauk counters that broader content support could avoid custom controls for multicolour, ThemeResource-aware icons, especially in menus. That is a useful real-world adoption argument, not consensus or an accepted platform commitment.

- **RadioButtons resize-memory report gets qualified good news.** Issue #7640, opened August 24, 2022; new reporter confirmation October 12. <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/7640#issuecomment-6116594637>
  Following yesterday’s retest discussion, the original reporter migrated the demo from UWP to WinUI 3, targeted 2.5.4-experimental, observed no obvious rapid-resize memory growth, and closed the issue. The changed framework/package matters: this supports that experimental WinUI 3 scenario, not proof that the original UWP case or a stable release was repaired.
