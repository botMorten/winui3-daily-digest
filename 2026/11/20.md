## Community experience

- **Wintodo: a new native, local-first task manager.** r/windowsapps, November 20. The author announces a WinUI 3/.NET 10 NativeAOT app for x64/ARM64 with SQLite/FTS5, CalDAV, read-only Microsoft Graph import, high-contrast support and reminders scheduled to fire after exit. Its source-available repository includes unusually detailed performance records: importantly, the advertised roughly 87 MB is **private working set**, versus roughly 182 MB total working set—not interchangeable figures. Benchmarks are author-measured, not independently reproduced; the author also acknowledges extensive AI-assisted coding. A concrete adoption example, not proof every WinUI app achieves its numbers.
  <https://www.reddit.com/r/windowsapps/comments/1y17hcz/wintodo_a_native_winui_3_replacement_for/>
  <https://github.com/Dvaderfun/wintodo>

- **ProtocolForResults remains a production migration blocker for one team.** WindowsAppSDK issue, originally December 2021; new November 20 report. A team says `LaunchUriForResultsAsync` from UWP to a packaged WinUI 3 target still fails on fully updated Windows 11 24H2. They distinguish their caller/target combination from an earlier successful OS-component-caller test and reject keeping a visible UWP shim window alive. No newly verified fix; do not generalize this to every activation scenario.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/1938#issuecomment-7026625938>

- **Two toast progress bars expose an API-migration gap.** WindowsAppSDK Discussion/issue, November 20. A developer migrating Toolkit notifications reports that the new progress-data bindings make two bars mirror one another rather than update independently. A community reply suggests keeping one bar static and replacing the toast with the same Tag/Group when that bar changes; that is heavier than an incremental update and remains an untested suggestion, not Microsoft guidance.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/6993>
  <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/discussions/6992#discussioncomment-19763805>

## News/releases

- **MSIX BuildTools 1.7.261119100: published, with a specific success report.** NuGet, November 19; reporter confirmation November 20. The package is now available, and the affected developer confirms it fixes their .NET 11 subfoldered NativeAOT duplicate `WinRTCOMServer.pdb` payload failure. Separately, the contributor says version strong-naming should prevent stale/mixed build-task DLL problems; that broader prevention claim has not been independently retested. This is packaging tooling, **not a new Windows App SDK runtime**.
  <https://www.nuget.org/packages/Microsoft.Windows.SDK.BuildTools.MSIX/1.7.261119100>
  <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/issues/6353#issuecomment-7029544713>

- **Missed catch-up: more reliable command-line activation for packaged apps.** Microsoft Inside MSIX, November 17. Howard Kapustein explains that a global AppExecutionAlias can collide or be disabled, whereas the package-family-specific alias under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Microsoft\WindowsApps\<PFN>\` avoids those global-alias problems. Useful for scripts integrating with packaged WinUI applications; general MSIX guidance, not a new WinUI API.
  <https://devblogs.microsoft.com/insidemsix/activation-via-command-line/>

## microsoft-ui-xaml — past 24 hours

**Exact window: November 19, 08:30 → November 20, 08:30 PST.** Direct API inspection covered 21 all-state issues, their comments/timelines, and all 650 current Discussions with paginated comments/replies.

- **TableView accessibility and keyboard editing: fixes merged November 20.** Issues from October 28 gained a source fix that makes recycled row/cell automation objects become unavailable instead of silently pointing at a different item. A related merged editing change improves Tab/Shift+Tab handling, cancellation and live-shaping coordination. Significant for accessible data-entry apps, but **not yet verified in a released package**.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12444>
  <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/pull/12656>

- **`Window.Activate` is not a background foreground-stealing guarantee.** The August 2022 issue received a November 19 explanation and closure: committed documentation clarifies the foreground-app constraint and Windows activation restrictions. JesseCol says experimental `Window.TrySetForeground` exists in main; shipping remains tracked separately. A contract clarification, not a new stable fix.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/7595#issuecomment-7020094328>

- **Photo replacement GPU-memory growth gets a controlled repro.** New November 19 issue: one reporter’s six-photo replacement workload grows GPU memory substantially; retaining removed images for five seconds reduces retained memory in two minimal-repro runs. Earlier experiments included an outlier. Treat delayed disposal as an investigation lead—not a reliable universal workaround or confirmed root cause.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12770>

- **Another memory report narrows to 198 versus 199 primitives.** The November 19 thread gained a new minimal repro that evening: its author reports a persistent roughly 65 MB allocation at 199 rectangles/SpriteVisuals on Snapdragon X Elite/Adreno. Earlier x64 full-app non-reproduction remains relevant; the minimal cross-hardware test is still outstanding. No fix established.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12759#issuecomment-7025626513>

- **Yesterday’s XAML build-action problem gains two proposed repair paths.** New November 19 cross-references link an SDK exclusion extension and a WindowsAppSDK proposal restoring classic VS item templates while preserving CLI templates. Both PRs remain open: useful progress, not a shipped repair.
  <https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-ui-xaml/issues/12758>
  <https://github.com/microsoft/WindowsAppSDK/pull/6991>
